# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a place where anyone can contribute — regardless of
experience, language, nationality, gender identity, appearance, disability,
religion, or the company they work for. Maintainers and contributors commit to
making participation harassment-free for everyone.

我们希望这个项目对任何人都能参与。以下是 Contributor Covenant 2.1 的要点，
完整文本见 <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>。

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about the work and generous with the person. Review the change, not the
  author.
- Assume competence: ask why a decision was made before calling it wrong.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change for reasons of scope or maintenance
  cost, not because the contribution was worthless.
- Give and take technical criticism gracefully; it is aimed at the code.
- Respect that people write in different languages and at different levels of
  comfort. Nobody is required to write perfect English.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and sustained disruption.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing somebody's private information without explicit permission.
- Dismissing a report of a problem because "it works on my machine" or because the
  reporter is unfamiliar with the codebase.

## Scope

This applies in the repository — issues, pull requests, commit messages, code
comments — and anywhere the project is represented, including chat and events.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to the repository owner, through the same private route
as [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md) describes. Reports are read by the maintainer and
kept confidential. Consequences escalate with severity: a private clarification, a
warning, a temporary ban from the repository, or a permanent ban. The maintainer
will say what was decided and why, to the reporter and to the person concerned.
